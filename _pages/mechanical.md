This is where software ends and physics begins. I've always been more
interested in understanding how things actually work than in having them work
by accident. That applies to code and to metal equally.

---

## Motorcycle Suspension

The main obsession. Suspension geometry, linkage ratios, spring rates,
damping curves — the interaction between rider, chassis, and road surface is
a genuinely complex dynamic system, and getting it right requires understanding
the whole chain, not just adjusting clickers.

Current and past work includes:
- Linkage ratio analysis and re-engineering
- Spring selection and preload calculation
- Damping setup and tuning methodology
- Geometry changes and their effects on handling balance

---

## Machining

Access to a lathe and mill means that when the right part doesn't exist, you
make it. Work has included custom suspension components, brackets, adapters,
and anything else that requires tighter tolerances than a welder with a grinder
can provide.

---

## General Fabrication

Welding, fitting, and fabricating mechanical assemblies. Not a trade
background, but a working knowledge built up from solving specific problems.
