## Audio tooling

Some of the audio work is closer to engineering than to playing. The most
involved projects include Apple Silicon drivers for the long-discontinued
E-MU USB audio interfaces and the M-Audio Fast Track Ultra. What started with
USB protocol archaeology and a test bench using a mixer I already owned has
grown into work on monitoring latency and a reusable audio driver development
template.

[Audio drivers and development tools →]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }})

[Reviving an E-MU Tracker Pre on Apple Silicon →]({{ '/projects/emu-apple-silicon/' | relative_url }})

I am participating in a project of restoring a Firewire support in modern versions of MacOS while also reviving a RME Fireface 800 audio interface.
